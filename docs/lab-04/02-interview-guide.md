# Discovery interview guide
Purpose: understand work before selecting a solution. Use only after instructor approval and participant consent. Approximately 15 minutes; avoid personal customer records, confidential transactions, and identifying third-party details.

## Eight neutral questions
1. How do you currently manage property information or promotional information from preparation through publication?
2. Where, if anywhere, do delays, errors, or repeated work occur in that process?
3. Who needs to find or use this information, and what are they trying to accomplish?
4. What information and content do you currently provide, and how do you decide what is needed?
5. Who checks content before publication, and how are corrections or disagreements handled?
6. How do you currently judge whether this work is successful?
7. What experiences have you or your audience had using this information on phones or with accessibility needs?
8. What existing tools, data sources, or technical constraints affect this work?

## Why these are neutral
The questions invite descriptions of current behavior, including the possibility that no problem exists. They do not assume a platform, validation feature, advertising workflow, or preferred answer. Answers can identify requirements, responsibilities, constraints, and success measures without asking for private customer information.
