## Invitation
Subject: Optional 15-minute CIS 3610 student discovery interview

Hello,

We are Team B — Beacon, students in CIS 3610 at California State University, Los Angeles. We are preparing a class project about how property professionals and local businesses manage property or promotional information.

With instructor approval, we would like to invite you to an approximately 15-minute discovery conversation about your current workflow, challenges, and content needs. Participation is voluntary. You may decline any question, decline the invitation, or stop at any time.

With your permission, we would take brief notes and use de-identified information in class planning documentation. Please avoid sharing customer identities, confidential business records, credentials, or sensitive transaction details. We would not record audio or video under this invitation. Class documents would omit your name and business affiliation; even de-identified details can sometimes be recognizable, so we would generalize unnecessary specifics.

Please participate only if this is comfortable for you. Thank you for considering our student project.

Team B — Beacon
CIS 3610, California State University, Los Angeles

## Separate consent statements
Before starting, record the participant's responses in a restricted team location, not this GitHub repository.
- Note-taking: “Do you consent to brief written notes during this conversation? You may ask us to stop taking notes at any time.”
- Class use: “Do you consent to use of de-identified and generalized information from these notes in our class requirements documents? You may decline this use independently of note-taking.”

No consent has been obtained. If either permission is declined, respect that choice and do not use material beyond the permission granted. Confirm instructor rules for retention, access, withdrawal requests, and deletion before collecting notes. Explain any withdrawal limits before consent. Do not promise absolute anonymity or a deletion policy the team cannot implement.
