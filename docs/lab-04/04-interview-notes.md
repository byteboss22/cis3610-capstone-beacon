# Simulated discovery notes
**SIMULATED DISCOVERY MATERIAL — NOT VERIFIED STAKEHOLDER INTERVIEW EVIDENCE**

Purpose: illustrate traceable planning until approved interviews or instructor-supplied personas exist. No real names, affiliations, quotations, consent, or interview dates are supplied or implied.

| ID | Stakeholder role | Simulated observation | Hypothetical pain point | Possible requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OBS-01 | Residential listing agent | Required property information is sometimes missing | Rework to complete a listing | Identify required fields and prevent incomplete submissions |
| OBS-02 | Residential listing agent | Property information is sometimes incorrectly formatted | Inconsistent or inaccurate listings | Explain format errors beside affected fields |
| OBS-03 | Local business sponsor | Sponsor wants to submit promotional information | Unclear submission expectations | Provide sponsor content instructions and submission capability |
| OBS-04 | Local business sponsor | Sponsor needs clarity about approvals | Unclear publishing responsibility and status | Define review authority and communicate decisions |

These rows restate or unpack the supplied hypothetical scenario. They are not observed behavior or interview findings.

## Unanswered questions
- Which listing fields and formats are necessary, and who maintains updates?
- How often do these problems occur, and what impact do they have?
- What promotional material is permitted and what evidence supports claims?
- Who approves each content type, and what happens when corrections are needed?
- What audience, device, accessibility, and integration needs actually exist?

## Replacement process
After approved discovery, replace or supplement rows with de-identified evidence IDs, consent status kept separately, and actual limitations. Preserve traceability and identify stories changed by new evidence. Instructor personas may replace these simulations; record their source without calling them interviews.
