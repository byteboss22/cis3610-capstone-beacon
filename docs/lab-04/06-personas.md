# Provisional personas
Purpose: support working discussions; both personas are **provisional working hypotheses**, not research-validated profiles. No demographic assumptions or real identities are included.

## Persona 1: Residential Listing Agent
- Role and responsibilities: prepare, update, and check residential property information.
- Goals: publish accurate listings and reduce correction work.
- Potential frustrations: missing information, inconsistent formats, unclear review outcomes (OBS-01/02; review frustration is an inference).
- Existing workflow: prepares information and updates listings; actual tools and handoffs are unknown.
- Desired improvements: clear required fields, actionable validation, editing, and visible review status.
- Technical comfort level: unknown; do not assume expertise or lack of skill. Confirm familiarity with forms and existing tools.
- Accessibility considerations: candidate need for keyboard access, clear labels, readable error messages, and mobile use; individual needs remain unknown.
- Evidence: OBS-01/02 support the hypothetical pain points; other details are AI inferences pending discovery.

## Persona 2: Local Business Sponsor
- Role and responsibilities: prepare promotional descriptions and keep business service information current.
- Goals: submit appropriate content and reach nearby customers.
- Potential frustrations: uncertain content requirements and approval responsibility (OBS-03/04).
- Existing workflow: wants to submit promotional information; actual tools, assets, and review steps are unknown.
- Desired improvements: submission guidance, clear status, correction requests, and a defined reviewer.
- Technical comfort level: unknown; confirm actual workflow and support needs.
- Accessibility considerations: candidate need for mobile access, keyboard controls, instructions outside color cues, and understandable feedback.
- Evidence: OBS-03/04 support submission and approval concerns; customer reach, workflow details, and access needs remain hypotheses.
