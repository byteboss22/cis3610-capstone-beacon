## Provisional one-page value proposition
**Audience:** property professionals managing residential listings and local sponsors promoting services. Public property visitors are a candidate secondary audience, not a confirmed interview group.

**Problem hypothesis:** incomplete or inconsistently formatted listing data may create rework; sponsors may not know what to submit or who approves it.

**Proposed value:** a clear submission process, explicit required information, helpful correction messages, and a shared review queue could reduce avoidable rework and make approved information easier to browse.

**Candidate offering:** basic listing display and detail pages, listing submission validation, and controlled content approval. Sponsor submission is a candidate extension requiring scope confirmation.

**Potential benefits:** fewer incomplete submissions, clearer responsibilities, and current public information. These are expected benefits, not measured outcomes.

**Differentiation hypothesis:** emphasize clear local content requirements and transparent review status. No competitor research has been conducted.

**Possible measures:** incomplete-submission rate, time from submission to review, correction turnaround, and stakeholder task completion. Baselines and target values are unknown.

**Constraints:** course timeline, instructor approval before outreach, minimal personal data, accessibility, and no functional implementation authorization yet.

**Validation:** use the interview guide to test actual workflows and needs; reject or revise ideas not supported by evidence. Confirm audience, ownership, fields, publishing rules, and Week 8 feasibility before implementation.
